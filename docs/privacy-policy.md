# Nourish Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

Nourish is a local-first nutrition and hydration tracker.

## Data stored on your device

Meals, nutrition estimates, water entries, goals, settings, and meal photos are
stored locally on your device. Nourish does not require an account and does not
upload your food history or photos to iCloud.

## Food lookup

Nourish uses its on-device food catalog first. When Open Food Facts lookup is
enabled and a local match is uncertain, the food search phrase is sent to Open
Food Facts to retrieve packaged-food nutrition data. Nourish does not send your
name, photos, meal history, or advertising identifier with that request.

Open Food Facts operates under its own privacy practices:
https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy

## Photos and camera

Photo access is used only when you choose or capture a meal photo. Photos remain
in Nourish storage on your device.

## Notifications

Hydration reminders are scheduled locally by iOS. Nourish does not use remote
push notifications.

## Tracking and advertising

Nourish does not display ads, create advertising profiles, sell personal data,
or track activity across other companies' apps and websites.

## Delete your data

Use Profile > Reset this device to delete data stored by Nourish. Deleting the
app also removes its local app data.

## Health disclaimer

Nutrition values are estimates for personal tracking and are not medical advice.
Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical or dietary decisions.
